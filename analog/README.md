# Comparator and clock generator (Sky130 `g5v0d10v5`, 3.3 V, ngspice)

Transistor-level replacements for the two blocks that were ideal in `dsm1/`. Everything is generated by `cells.py`, which
imports the device emitter from `v2/sim/v2.py`, so models and naming match the rest of the repository. **Schematic-level
simulation only: no layout, no extraction, no mismatch or noise.**

## Blocks

**Comparator** (`cells.strongarm`): StrongARM latch (input pair 8/0.5 um, tail 8/0.5, cross-coupled 4/0.5 n and 8/0.5 p, four 2/0.5 reset
PMOS), then two 1/2 um inverters that isolate the latch outputs, a NOR SR latch (1/4 um) that holds the decision through the reset
phase, and 8/24 um output buffers. 50 fF on each latch output as a placeholder wiring load. Evaluates while `cc` is high.

**Clock generator** (`cells.clockgen`): one 50 % master clock in; `p1a`, `p1`, `p2` (and complements) and the comparator clock `cc` out.
Cross-coupled NOR non-overlap generator with long-L delay chains for the dead time, the early opening of `p1a` (bottom-plate
sampling) and the delay of `cc`.

## What was found and fixed: comparator hysteresis

The first comparator (StrongARM + NAND SR latch fed directly from the latch outputs) showed **about +/-3.3 to 3.75 mV of hysteresis**
at every corner: the decision depended on the previous one. Removing the SR latch (core only, raw outputs) gave no hysteresis within the
1 mV sweep step, so the memory came from the SR latch loading the two core outputs unequally depending on its state. Putting a
small inverter between each core output and the latch (NOR latch, active-high inputs) removes it. The earlier version is still
available as `cells.strongarm(..., iso=None)` and its corner results are kept in `results/analog/comp_corners_nand_latch.jsonl`.
Cost: about 20 % more supply current (80 -> 97 uW at TT).

Test (`tb_comp.py`): the differential input is ramped up and then down in 0.5 mV steps, one step per clock; hysteresis is the difference
between the switching point on the way up and on the way down. **The resolution of this test is 0.5 mV, so "0.5 mV" below means
"not resolved", not a measured 0.5 mV.**

| Corner | Hysteresis, NAND latch (earlier) | Hysteresis, isolated NOR latch (current) | Max decision delay | Supply current | Power |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TT / 27 C / 3.3 V | 6.50 mV | 0.50 mV | 2.7 ns | 29.31 uA | 96.7 uW |
| SS / 125 C / 3.0 V | 6.50 mV | 0.50 mV | 3.9 ns | 15.39 uA | 46.2 uW |
| FF / -40 C / 3.6 V | 6.50 mV | 0.00 mV | 2.1 ns | 46.91 uA | 168.9 uW |
| SF / 27 C / 3.3 V | 7.50 mV | 0.50 mV | 2.6 ns | 31.88 uA | 105.2 uW |
| FS / 27 C / 3.3 V | 5.50 mV | 0.50 mV | 2.8 ns | 26.12 uA | 86.2 uW |

Single transition up and down, complementary outputs (q + qb = Vdd) and no missed decisions at all five corners.

## Clock generator corners (`tb_clk.py`, with the modulator's switch loads attached)

| Corner | p2 fall -> p1a rise | p1 fall -> p2 rise | p1a open before p1 | cc rise after p2 fall | Supply current |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TT / 27 C / 3.3 V | 7.05 ns | 7.03 ns | 7.23 ns | 12.83 ns | 48.88 uA |
| SS / 125 C / 3.0 V | 11.52 ns | 11.5 ns | 11.86 ns | 21.44 ns | 28.89 uA |
| FF / -40 C / 3.6 V | 4.33 ns | 4.32 ns | 4.45 ns | 7.84 ns | 81.35 uA |
| SF / 27 C / 3.3 V | 7.63 ns | 7.6 ns | 7.75 ns | 13.37 ns | 48.28 uA |
| FS / 27 C / 3.3 V | 6.68 ns | 6.66 ns | 6.93 ns | 12.68 ns | 48.89 uA |

Dead time is positive at every corner (smallest 4.3 ns, FF/-40 C/3.6 V). `p1a` always opens before `p1`.

## Reproduce

```
make comparator        # about 15 s, TT
make analog-corners    # about 4 min, five corners, both testbenches
```

Results: `results/analog/comp_corners.jsonl`, `results/analog/clk_corners.jsonl`.

## Limits of this evidence

* Deterministic: **no mismatch (Monte Carlo), no thermal noise, no kickback onto a real reference.** The input-referred offset of a real
  device pair is not covered; the 0.25 mV above is the circuit's systematic offset only.
* Five corners, one supply and temperature per corner, 1.024 MHz only.
* In the modulator loop the comparator decision differs from the sign of `out - vcm` by up to about 25 mV in some cycles, because it
  evaluates 30-60 ns after the switches change phase, while the integrator output and the `vcm` reference are still settling (see `dsm1/README.md`).
* `dbg.py` and `var.py` are the debugging scripts used while investigating the hysteresis and are kept for the record; `var.py` predates the fix.
